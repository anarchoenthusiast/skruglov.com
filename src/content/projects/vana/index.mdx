Wearables already collect more about your health than most people ever look at. The R&D brief for Vana was about the next step: how to raise the value of insights for the user, how to shape UX so the experience feels maximally seamless, and how to increase social interaction within the audience.

I explored a few directions. Insights could live in cards with interactive graphics per data type: a weekly recovery view, a longevity estimate, an update people want to share.
</ProjectNote>
