Ai Video Cut turns long videos into short clips. As part of a two-founder team, I developed the product concept, UX and visual identity. We released the first version in six weeks.

After launch, I developed the design system and continued working on the product's visual design. In its first six months, Ai Video Cut reached 25,000+ users and grew by about 12% per month.

Users can find clips with a text prompt, edit the transcript, adjust the crop and add subtitles. My work covered these steps and the export options, including support for different languages and formats.
