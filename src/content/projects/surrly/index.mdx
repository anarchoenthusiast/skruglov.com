Surrly is a solo-built iOS dream journal powered by AI. I designed, coded, and launched the product myself over three months in the summer of 2025, taking it from a product idea to a working app in TestFlight.

The project was a way to show the full builder loop from the engineering side: turning a product thesis into app architecture, implementing the iOS experience, wiring AI-powered interpretation flows, handling release details, and shipping a real build instead of stopping at a prototype.

I also used an AI-assisted development workflow to move faster across implementation, copy iteration, debugging, and product decisions. For me, Surrly is a compact proof that I can bridge product, design, code, release process, and DevOps-minded execution as one builder.
