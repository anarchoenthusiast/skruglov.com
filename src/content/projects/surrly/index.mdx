Most people lose a dream within minutes of waking up. Surrly was a summer side project: a gentle iOS journal for capturing dreams and reading them back with AI. I built it alone over three months in 2025. The app is on the App Store now, and the audience is still small, but it keeps growing.

The core loop had to feel quiet, not like a productivity app. Voice or text, a few taps, done. The home screen shows recent dreams without pushing you to write more than you want.
</ProjectNote>

AI interpretation was the part I was unsure about. I wanted readings that felt thoughtful, not like generic horoscope copy. Each entry pulls themes and symbols from what you actually wrote, not a fixed template.
</ProjectNote>

I used Cursor and other AI tools heavily while building, but the release work was still mine: App Store listing, onboarding, subscriptions, crash fixes. The project was a test of whether I could take something from idea to a live app people would download.
