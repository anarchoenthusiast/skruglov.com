Surrly is an AI-powered dream journal for iOS. The idea was simple: give people a gentle companion to record dreams and surface meaning from them — without the friction of traditional journaling apps.

I built the product entirely on my own over the summer of 2025. That meant defining the concept, designing the experience, writing the copy, building and shipping the app, and getting it in front of real users through TestFlight. Every decision — product, brand, interaction — was mine to make and mine to own.

The name comes from that in-between feeling: not quite awake, not quite asleep. The tone of the product follows that same logic. Nothing harsh, nothing clinical. Just a calm surface that gets out of the way and lets you capture something before it disappears.
