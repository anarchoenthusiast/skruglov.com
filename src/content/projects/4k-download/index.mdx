My main period at 4K Download was 2012–2020. This case focuses on the desktop redesign work in 2016–2020 and includes subsequent project contributions. I redesigned five complex applications for Windows, macOS, and Ubuntu, bringing more structure to interfaces that carried years of legacy decisions.

By 2020, I had formulated hypotheses about market demand and defined core features for 4K Tokkit and 4K Image Compressor. This was concept work ahead of their later public launches.

I built a team of three designers and created a design system with 100+ components, reducing the UI release cycle from approximately three weeks to ten days. Using Figma and AI, I reduced asset preparation time from approximately five days to two; the AI-assisted work was part of my later project involvement.

Across the desktop work, I proposed an embedded browser and a shared control panel, and integrated AI post-processing. These features and workflows improved activation and retention. Through experimentation, paywall conversion increased from 3% to 6.8%.

The product family has a cumulative user base of over 60 million across 190 countries.

4K Video Downloader ranked No. 1 in [TechRadar's roundup of free YouTube downloaders](https://www.techradar.com/best/free-youtube-downloader), and was featured in PCMag and Digital Trends.
