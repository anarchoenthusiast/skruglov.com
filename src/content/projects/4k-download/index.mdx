At 4K Download, I worked on desktop apps, the website and subscriptions.

<h2 id="desktop-applications">Desktop applications</h2>

I redesigned five apps for Windows, macOS and Ubuntu. Their interfaces had changed over many years, so I focused on making the screens and controls more consistent.

By 2020, I had developed concepts for 4K Tokkit and 4K Image Compressor: who might need them and which features they should offer. Both products launched later.

I built a team of three designers and a design system with 100+ components. This shortened the UI release cycle from about three weeks to ten days. In later work on the project, I used Figma and AI tools to cut the time needed to prepare graphics from about five days to two.

I proposed an in-app browser and a shared control panel, and worked on adding AI post-processing. These changes helped improve activation and retention. We also tested the paywall, increasing conversion from 3% to 6.8%.

More than 60 million people in 190 countries have used 4K Download products.

4K Video Downloader ranked first in [TechRadar's list of free YouTube downloaders](https://www.techradar.com/best/free-youtube-downloader). PCMag and Digital Trends also covered it.
