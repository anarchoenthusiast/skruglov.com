Waveroom lets people record podcasts and interviews remotely in the browser. I designed the recording workflows and a design system for web, iOS and desktop.

The flows had to account for local recording, codec differences between devices, latency, dropped connections and file processing after a session. I designed the steps people would follow and built components for recording and working with media.

I built a token-based design system. The visual identity I developed covered more than 20 touchpoints, including app interfaces and marketing materials.
