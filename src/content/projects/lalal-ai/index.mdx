LALAL.AI needed to turn complex AI audio processing into a product millions of people could understand and use with confidence. As founding designer from 2020 to 2026, I helped shape the product from its earliest stage and turn that direction into a scalable experience across web, mobile, and desktop.

My role covered product direction, brand foundations, interface design, and the system behind the experience. I created the visual standard that unified the platform and a design system with 70+ components spanning web, mobile, desktop, and marketing. That product language supported growth without losing clarity or consistency.

As the company expanded, I built a team of four designers, introduced DesignOps practices, and made product work more repeatable. By 2025, LALAL.AI had reached 6.8 million registered users; in 2025, the service performed 63.8 million audio separation operations. One of my key contributions was proposing and validating Voice Cleaner, which accounted for approximately 14% of LALAL.AI's revenue over one quarter.
