LALAL.AI separates vocals and individual instruments from audio recordings. I joined at the start and designed the main steps: uploading a file, listening to a preview and downloading the separated tracks.

Product data and interviews showed that people were uploading speech recordings with no music. I proposed Voice Cleaner as a separate tool for cleaning up speech and tested demand for it. Over one quarter, it accounted for about 14% of LALAL.AI's revenue.

As the product grew, I built a team of four designers, set up the team's design process and created a design system with 70+ components for the web, mobile and desktop apps, and marketing materials. By 2025, LALAL.AI had 6.8 million registered users. The service completed 63.8 million audio separation operations in 2025.
