<h2 id="starting-point" className="scroll-mt-6">Make AI separation easy to try</h2>

The first challenge was to make unfamiliar technology useful without explaining how it worked. I built the experience around a clear promise: upload a recording, separate vocals from music, and hear the result.

That flow became the foundation for new stem types and products as LALAL.AI grew.

<h2 id="voice-cleaner" className="scroll-mt-6">Turn unexpected usage into a new product</h2>

Product data and interviews revealed a pattern: people were uploading speech recordings with no music. I saw an opportunity to serve voice cleanup as a dedicated use case and proposed Voice Cleaner.

We tested demand with a separate product page and promotion through our marketing channels. Usage and revenue showed that people used the feature and paid for it. Voice Cleaner accounted for approximately 14% of LALAL.AI's revenue over one quarter.

Voice Cleaner launched in 2022. My contribution covered the initial insight, product proposal, user journey, and demand validation. [Launch report](https://www.lalal.ai/blog/lalalai-2022-wrapped/).

<h2 id="core-decisions" className="scroll-mt-6">Remove friction from listening and sign-in</h2>

Under my design leadership, the team used interviews, a survey of 1,000+ users, and journey mapping to prioritize improvements. Two changes made the core journey easier.

### Hear the mix before processing

We brought simultaneous playback, independent levels, stereo controls, and seeking into the preview. People could evaluate the mix before processing the full file.

### Keep sign-in in the same session

The team replaced email links with a six-digit code and gave social sign-in equal prominence. This let people continue in the same browser or app. The project designer reported 20% more registrations after the redesign.

<h2 id="ecosystem" className="scroll-mt-6">Scale the design across products and platforms</h2>

As the product grew, my role expanded from hands-on founding design to leading four designers. I introduced DesignOps and created a design system with 70+ components spanning web, mobile, desktop, and marketing. I established the brand and interface foundations, designed the core journeys, and guided feature work and art direction across web, desktop, mobile, and the DAW plugin.

The challenge was to carry the same product language into different workflows: a file queue on desktop, listening on mobile, and separation inside music-production software.
