<ProjectNote>
The MVP was closer to a one-button app than a wallet. A grid of holdings with live gains and losses, then one tap into a flip sheet: swap pair, a short countdown, hold to confirm. More r/WallStreetBets than Uniswap.
</ProjectNote>

<Image src={img4} alt="Flipp — swap widget expanding from a community post" loading="lazy" quality={80} widths={[700, 1400]} sizes="(max-width: 768px) 100vw, 700px" />

<ProjectNote>
You do not start a flip from a portfolio screen. You see a token in a thread, a card slides up from the feed, opens into a chart, and you flip from there. The context stays social.
</ProjectNote>
