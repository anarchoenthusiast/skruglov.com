My main period at 4K Download was 2012–2020, with later project involvement. In 2024–2025, as Head of Design, I owned website and subscription design. My scope covered the marketing site, subscription surfaces, and the design system that would keep marketing and product aligned. The product family has a cumulative user base of over 60 million across 190 countries.
